# Keel — Symptom List (Build-Ready Spec)

**Version:** v1.0 draft for review
**Owner:** Mischa · **Build:** Chunru
**What this is:** The final symptom set for the daily check-in: labels, gentle group headings, and what shows by default versus what sits behind "more". Australian spelling throughout. Built to the recognition-before-education principle: the default view stays small and common, the rest is discoverable, and nothing overwhelms her.

## How to read the flags

- **[D]** = default chip. Shown immediately in the check-in (the small, fast set).
- No flag = lives in the "more" picker, grouped as below. Available, not in her face.
- **[sensitive]** = lives in the opt-in "Intimacy & bladder" group, introduced gently.
- Every group ends with **+ Add your own** (custom entry, saved for reuse).

## The default chips (shown first, before "more")

Keep this set small so the check-in stays a 15-second exhale. These are the common, validating, low-sensitivity ones:

Hot flushes [D] · Night sweats [D] · Trouble sleeping [D] · Fatigue or low energy [D] · Brain fog [D] · Headache [D] · Joint pain [D] · Anxious [D] · Irritable [D] · Low mood [D] · Mood swings [D] · Not feeling like myself [D]

After about two weeks of check-ins, re-order the default chips to surface her most-used first. The list adapts to her, not the other way around.
