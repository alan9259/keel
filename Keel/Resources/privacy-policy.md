**Last updated:** 3 September 2026
**Applies to:** the Keel iOS UAT app and associated services ("Keel", "we", "us")

### Who we are

Keel is provided by TRY Keel Pty Ltd (ACN 701 528 467, ABN 50 701 528 467), based in Melbourne, Australia.

Keel is a companion app for women navigating perimenopause. This policy explains what personal information Keel collects, why we collect it, how it is handled, and the choices you have.

Keel supports and informs. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace your doctor. Nothing in Keel is medical advice.

### What we collect

**Information you choose to give Keel**

Depending on the features you use, this may include:
- an optional name and email address
- mood, energy, symptoms and other daily check-in information
- notes you type or record by voice
- HRT, medication, treatment and supplement information you choose to record
- cycle information
- information about changes in your treatment or approach

Keel does not collect login credentials. An Apple sign-in/session is optional.

Keel does not currently store lab results.

Much of the information you choose to record is health information and is treated as sensitive information.

**Information from Apple Health**

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Keel may read the categories you specifically authorise. These can include:
- sleep
- steps and other activity information
- exercise minutes
- active energy
- flights climbed
- walking or running distance
- heart rate
- resting heart rate
- heart rate variability
- blood pressure
- respiratory rate
- blood oxygen
- body temperature
- wrist temperature
- basal body temperature
- body weight
- menstrual flow
- mindful minutes
- supported symptoms recorded in Apple Health, such as hot flushes or night sweats

Keel only accesses Apple Health categories that you approve. You can change those permissions through Apple Health or your iPhone privacy settings.

Keel does not write health information back to Apple Health.

Information obtained through Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing and is never sold.

**Device and diagnostic information**

Keel may receive limited technical information needed to operate, secure and improve the app, such as app version, crash information and general performance diagnostics. We do not use this information for advertising.

### Why we collect your information

We use information only where needed to provide and operate Keel, including to:
- provide your check-ins and other features
- show your information back to you over time
- support treatment and medication logging
- generate your GP summary
- maintain the security and reliability of the app
- respond to privacy or support requests

We do not sell your personal or health information. We do not use your health information for advertising or marketing.

### Voice notes and transcription

If you choose to use a voice note, Keel uses Apple's speech recognition technology to turn your spoken words into text.

Keel currently uses SFSpeechRecognizer. Because on-device recognition is not required in this UAT build, audio may be sent securely to Apple for speech recognition processing, depending on the device and service availability.

You can review and edit the transcription before it is saved.

Keel only listens when you choose to use the voice feature.

### Your GP summary

Your GP summary is generated from information you have recorded in Keel using a fixed format.

There is no artificial intelligence involved in generating the UAT GP summary and no automated assessment or diagnosis of your health.

The summary organises information you have already recorded so you can review it and decide whether to share it with a healthcare professional.

Keel does not interpret the summary as a diagnosis, prescribe treatment or tell you what your symptoms mean.

### Where your information is stored during UAT

During this testing period, information you record in Keel is stored locally on your iPhone.

iCloud/CloudKit sync is disabled for the UAT build while Keel completes further technical and privacy checks.

Keel does not operate its own server storing a copy of your health information.

Your information stays on your device unless you choose to export or share something, such as your GP summary.

### Apple services

Keel uses Apple technologies to provide parts of the app. These may include:
- HealthKit, if you choose to connect Apple Health
- Apple speech recognition, if you use voice transcription
- Apple diagnostic and App Store services

Apple handles information processed through its services under Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

### Who we share information with

Keel does not sell your personal information.

We do not provide your health information to advertisers, data brokers or marketing platforms.

Your GP summary is shared only when you choose to export, send, print or show it to someone.

Some information may be processed by Apple when you use Apple Health, speech recognition or other Apple services.

We may disclose information where we are required to do so by law. Where legally permitted and reasonably practicable, we will tell you about such a request.

### Health information and privacy laws

Information about your symptoms, cycle, treatments, medications and other health matters is sensitive health information.

TRY Keel Pty Ltd handles personal and health information in accordance with applicable privacy and health information laws in Australia and New Zealand.

These may include the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles, relevant state and territory health privacy laws including the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic), and the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and applicable health information privacy requirements.

We collect and use health information only where it is needed to provide the Keel features you choose to use and in accordance with the permissions and protections that apply.

### Your choices and rights

You can:
- view information you have recorded in Keel
- correct or update information
- change Apple Health permissions at any time
- choose whether to use voice transcription
- choose whether to create or share a GP summary
- request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you
- delete information stored in Keel
- ask us how your information is handled

To exercise these rights or ask a privacy question, contact us at keel@therecalibrationyears.com.

### Deleting your information

During UAT, your Keel data is stored locally on your iPhone. When you delete your Keel data in the app, the local data is deleted immediately.

Because iCloud/CloudKit sync is disabled for this UAT build, Keel does not create a CloudKit copy of your UAT health information.

We do not retain personal information longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to any legal obligations that require us to retain particular information.

### Keeping your information secure

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the information handled through Keel against misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

No technology can eliminate every security risk, but protecting health information is a core part of how Keel is designed.

If an eligible data breach occurs, we will respond in accordance with applicable privacy and data breach notification requirements.

### Overseas processing

Some Apple services, including speech recognition and other Apple infrastructure, may process information outside Australia or New Zealand.

Where applicable, we will handle personal information in accordance with the privacy requirements that apply to Keel.

### Privacy questions and complaints

If you have a question, concern or complaint about how Keel handles your information, please contact keel@therecalibrationyears.com.

Please give us enough information to understand your concern. We will investigate privacy complaints and aim to respond within a reasonable period.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may also be able to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), the Victorian Health Complaints Commissioner where the Victorian Health Records Act applies, or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner New Zealand.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Keel changes.

If we make a material change to how we collect, use or share personal information, we will make the updated policy available and, where appropriate, notify you before the change takes effect.
